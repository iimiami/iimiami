The house

Close to it all, in a house that is new.

The plan. The great room rises to a 20-foot ceiling, with 10-foot ceilings through the rest of the first floor. The primary suite is downstairs with a walk-in closet and a double-vanity bath, and so is a guest bedroom with its own full bath off the foyer. Upstairs: two more bedrooms, two baths, a bonus room and a deck over the lanai. Laundry on both floors.

The kitchen. A 12-foot island, a 48-inch dual-fuel range and hood, a 42-inch built-in refrigerator, wine refrigerator, quartz counters, solid wood Shaker cabinetry and a pantry. It opens straight to the dining area and the covered lanai, with its outdoor kitchen: gas grill, sink and refrigerator.

Built for Florida. Not in a flood zone. Solid block construction on both floors, impact windows and doors, a metal roof. Generator-ready, with the 500-gallon propane tank already in, so the house keeps running after a storm. Elevator-ready, with the shaft framed and pre-wired on both floors. Lutron smart home, wired security, dual-zone AC, tankless gas water heater.

Your finishes. A buyer under contract early chooses the paint, flooring and finishes.
