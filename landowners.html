Landowners & churches

Keep your land working.

Your land can build homes and income for your church or family, without selling it for a one-time check.

Before you sign anything

Three things to avoid.

01

Selling for a one-time check

A sale ends your stake in your own ground.

02

Giving up ownership with no way back

Every deal should show how you can own it all in the end.

03

Putting your church or credit at risk

Only the land you choose to contribute should be part of the project, and you should never sign a loan guarantee.

What a good deal looks like

Your land, your stake, your path to ownership.

Your land becomes your stake. Instead of selling, you contribute your land to the project and own a share of it from day one.

A clear path to owning it all. Rental communities: you can buy out the developer once the community is built and leased. Homes for sale: keep some homes or share in the sales.

Income for your mission. A share of the community's income, and homes for your church family, staff and seniors.

How it works

Four steps, at your pace.

1. Talk with us. No cost and no obligation. We look at your land and listen to what you hope for.

2. See your options. Rental or for-sale, what can be built, and what you would own.

3. Your attorney reviews everything. Nothing moves until your own counsel is satisfied.

4. We build it. II MIAMI arranges the financing, signs the loan guarantees and builds through our own licensed contractor, J & C Premier Builders (CBC #1266533). You share the income and keep your path to full ownership.
